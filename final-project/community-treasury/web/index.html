A SIMPLE WORKFLOW

From idea to payment

  1. 1
    Submit a request

    A manager specifies who receives how much and why.

  2. 2
    Collect two approvals

    Two different managers approve the same request. An approval can be revoked before payment.

  3. 3
    Execute once

    A manager sends the payment transaction. The contract checks approvals and available funds.

Managers judge whether an expense is appropriate. The contract enforces their approval rule; it does not verify work quality.
